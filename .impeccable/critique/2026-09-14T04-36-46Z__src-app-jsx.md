### Overall Impression
A charming, thoughtful, and technically sound digital gift with authentic emotional warmth. It elevates simple web content into a tactile memory space, though small accessibility gaps in modals and mobile touch interactions prevent it from feeling effortless.

---

### What's Working
1. **Skeuomorphic Craft & Physical Metaphors:** The cassette reels with rotating spools, vintage airmail envelopes with wax seals and postal stamps, and washi tape pins feel lovingly crafted.
2. **Resilient Ambient Sound Engine:** Using Web Audio API to synthesize pentatonic acoustic chimes when external audio is blocked guarantees the celebratory atmosphere never breaks.
3. **Palette Cohesion:** The implementation of Sky Mist (`#D4F1FF`) as the primary base color feels unified across canvas texture, tapes, and UI accents without overwhelming the content.

### Priority Issues

#### [P1] Missing Focus Trap & ARIA Dialog Semantics on Modals
- **Why it matters:** Keyboard and screen-reader users can tab straight out of an open letter into background elements, losing their place in the modal.
- **Fix:** Add `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` to `LetterModal` and `AudioWelcomeModal`, trap Tab navigation inside the open dialog, and return focus to the trigger on close.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden src/components/LetterModal.jsx`

#### [P1] Polaroid Flip Affordance Hidden on Touch Devices
- **Why it matters:** On mobile devices where hover does not exist, the flip cue is invisible (`group-hover:opacity-100`), meaning mobile users never discover the handwritten notes on the back of the photos.
- **Fix:** Add a persistent corner peel or subtle "Tap to flip" stamp on touch screens, and add `aria-expanded={isFlipped}` for screen readers.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt src/components/PolaroidCard.jsx`

#### [P2] Mechanical Easing & Slop Antipatterns (Detector Findings)
- **Why it matters:** Using stock Tailwind `animate-bounce` on the candle smoke puff and equalizer bars creates a jerky, toy-like motion curve rather than smooth organic dissipation or rhythmic audio activity.
- **Fix:** Replace `animate-bounce` on smoke with a rising float-and-fade keyframe, and use staggered height keyframes on the audio equalizer bars.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish src/components/BirthdayCake.jsx`

#### [P2] Incongruous Sticky Floating SaaS Navbar
- **Why it matters:** A floating pill header with backdrop blur belongs to a modern SaaS dashboard or tech portfolio, breaking the physical illusion of a tabletop scrapbook.
- **Fix:** Restyle navigation into scrapbook bookmarks or taped tabs pinned to the page edge, and tuck the music controls into the cassette deck or a vintage badge.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable delight src/components/Navbar.jsx`

#### [P2] Information Architecture Inversion
- **Why it matters:** Placing the mechanical cassette player above the photo gallery delays the emotional connection of seeing friends' faces and reading messages.
- **Fix:** Move the photo gallery directly beneath the hero header so memories lead the experience, and position the cassette player alongside or integrated into the board.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout src/components/ScrapbookBoard.jsx`

### Persona Red Flags

#### Jordan (First-Timer Celebrant / Friend)
- Scrolls through the Polaroids on a phone and assumes they are static images, completely missing the handwritten memory notes on the back.
- Encounters an audio modal immediately upon landing, which feels like a consent dialog before seeing the gift.

#### Sam (Accessibility & Keyboard User)
- Opens a letter with Enter key, then presses Tab and unexpectedly navigates behind the modal into the next envelope.
- Screen reader receives no announcement when a Polaroid flips to reveal the secret note.

#### Casey (Mobile One-Handed Celebrant)
- Sticky floating navbar occupies thumb space throughout vertical scrolling on small screens.
- Cassette volume slider hit target is too narrow for comfortable one-handed thumb interaction.
