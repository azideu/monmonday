### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Empty Polaroid Image Placeholders**
  - **Why it matters**: The memories gallery is the opening act. Leading with 6 identical dashed camera placeholder frames creates an "unfinished" impression and dilutes the immediate emotional impact before photos are supplied.
  - **Fix**: Provide charming illustrated SVG doodles or pastel vignettes (campfire, road trip car, pizza in the park, rooftop cake, arcade claw machine) as default backdrops so cards feel complete even before user photos are dropped in.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable delight`

- **[P2] Visual & Performance Clash: WebGL Fluid Cursor**
  - **Why it matters**: The high-velocity particle fluid simulation belongs to a high-tech demo and clashes with the warm, nostalgic paper stationery theme. Furthermore, calculating 1440-resolution dye physics on every mousemove strains GPUs on older laptops and drains mobile batteries.
  - **Fix**: Replace the WebGL fluid shader with an analog stationery trail (e.g. gentle drifting pastel star dust or watercolor droplets), or default it to disabled with a discreet "Magic Dust" toggle.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

- **[P2] Monolithic 10-Envelope Grid Hierarchy**
  - **Why it matters**: 10 identical envelope cards are laid out in a flat flex-wrap grid. The secret letter from akari and milestone letters blend in uniformly, creating subtle decision paralysis for the reader on where to begin.
  - **Fix**: Elevate the secret letter or feature a curated starting envelope ("Open First") with a distinct wax seal accent, or introduce thematic groupings ("From the Stream Crew", "Late Night Talks").
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P3] Distracting Infinite Bounce Animation on Unlocked Secret Letter**
  - **Why it matters**: Flagged by deterministic detector (`src/components/SecretLetterModal.jsx:134`). The `animate-bounce` utility loops indefinitely on the unlock icon above the letter, creating constant motion while the user attempts to read intimate personal prose.
  - **Fix**: Replace `animate-bounce` with a single celebratory entry pop (e.g. `<motion.div initial={{ scale: 0.8 }} animate={{ scale: [0.8, 1.15, 1] }} transition={{ ease: [0.16, 1, 0.3, 1], duration: 0.45 }}>`) that settles gracefully.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P3] Mobile Cassette Scrubber Touch Density**
  - **Why it matters**: On narrow mobile screens (<380px), the cassette tape scrubber, playhead thumb, transport controls, and volume slider are tightly packed into a 280px card, risking mis-taps.
  - **Fix**: Expand the interactive touch target of the playhead thumb to 44×44px (using transparent padding/hitbox) and stack the volume slider below the main buttons on small viewports.
