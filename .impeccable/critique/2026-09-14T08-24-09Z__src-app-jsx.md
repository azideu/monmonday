### Priority Issues

#### [P1] Micro-Type Legibility on Margin Postmarks & Tickets
- **Why it matters**: `ScrapbookDecoMargin.jsx` uses literal font sizes as small as `text-[6px]` and `text-[7px]` on cancellation rings and ticket numbers. On standard 1x DPI desktop monitors or high-scale mobile zooms, these become unreadable visual noise.
- **Fix**: Bump the minimum floor to `10px` (`text-[10px]`) and use opacity or font-mono uppercase tracking to convey vintage stamped character without sacrificing readability.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

#### [P2] Audio State & Volume Persistence Across Refresh
- **Why it matters**: When the celebrant refreshes the page or reopens the link, the welcome modal always re-prompts and volume resets to 70%, which can interrupt listening flow if they already set preferences.
- **Fix**: Store `hasInteracted` and `preferredVolume` in `localStorage` so returning to the page feels seamless and remembers their choice.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

#### [P3] Polaroid Backside Touch Affordance Clarity
- **Why it matters**: On touchscreens without hover, first-time users might not immediately realize the polaroids flip over in 3D to reveal secret handwritten memories on the back.
- **Fix**: Elevate the small "Flip" corner pill with a subtle soft pulse or an initial gentle tilt peek on first scroll into view.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable delight`
